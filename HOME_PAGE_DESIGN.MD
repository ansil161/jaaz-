1. Hero

Purpose: Create the first emotional impression of JAZ.
Full-screen theatre visual, strong headline, short supporting line, and primary CTA.

Headline:
“Entertainment without comfort is just noise.”

2. The Promise

Purpose: Introduce JAZ's core philosophy.
A minimal statement section explaining that true luxury entertainment must also deliver comfort.

Statement:
“Luxury without comfort is not luxury.”

3. The JAZ Comfort System

Purpose: Explain what makes JAZ different.
Three core pillars:

Visual Comfort — picture, projection and viewing experience
Acoustic Comfort — sound, acoustics and calibration
Seating Comfort — recliners, ergonomics and body comfort

This is the main “why JAZ?” section.

4. Bring the Big Screen Home

Purpose: Introduce the JAZ brand and company.
A strong image, short brand introduction, key statistics/achievements and a Discover More → About Us CTA.

This answers: “Who is JAZ?”

5. Spaces — What We Bring Alive

Purpose: Show the different experiences JAZ can create.

Private Theatre
Living Room
Gaming
Party
Bar
Terrace

Each space gets a strong image and a short description.

This answers: “What can JAZ create for me?”

6. From Setup to Showtime

Purpose: Show the craftsmanship and expertise behind every project.

3D Design
Wall Acoustics
Acoustic Flooring
Luxury Recliners
Star-Light Ceilings

A compact visual showcase rather than five separate sections.

This answers: “What goes into creating the experience?”

7. Technology & Brands

Purpose: Establish technical credibility.

Show the main technology categories:

Projectors · Screens · Speakers · Amplifiers · Subwoofers · Streaming · Control · Lighting · Seating

Then display the key brands JAZ works with through a clean logo wall.

This answers: “What technology powers the experience?”

8. Our Process

Purpose: Explain how JAZ takes a project from idea to completion.

01 Consultation → 02 Site Study → 03 Design → 04 Installation → 05 Calibration → 06 Handover

This makes the process feel professional, clear and trustworthy.

9. Testimonials

Purpose: Build trust through real customer experiences.

Show 3 strong testimonials with customer/project context and an option to view more.

This answers: “Can I trust JAZ?”

10. Final CTA

Purpose: Turn interest into an enquiry.

Headline:
“Design your private entertainment world.”

Short supporting text + Book a Consultation CTA.

Keep this section simple and powerful.

11. Footer

Purpose: Provide navigation and contact information.

Include:

JAZ logo
Main navigation
Contact details
Office location
Social links
Privacy / Terms
Final brand statement
